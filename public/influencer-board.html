Equity structure
Two pools. Yours to access first.

Both pools open before Mind raises external capital.

Performance pool: 2% equity

This pool rewards influencers whose introductions directly or indirectly led to a signed company deal. Deals don't have to be closed by you. If your company signed, your involvement is considered.

Allocation from this pool is at Mind's discretion, based on depth of involvement. There is no guarantee of allocation, but every confirmed influencer is eligible.

Purchase pool: 2% equity

Every confirmed influencer is offered the right to purchase a pro-rata share of 2% equity (i.e. 2% divided by the total number of influencers) before any external funding round.

If you choose not to purchase, your allocated share becomes available for other influencers to request. The portal tracks availability and requests, and Mind makes the final call on who buys.

Important: Equity access under both pools is contingent on confirmation as an influencer, at Mind's sole discretion. Joining the Board of Influencers does not constitute a guarantee of equity allocation or purchase rights. All formal terms will be governed by legal agreements made available prior to any transaction.
